# Simple Practice Journal — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Simple Practice Journal helps you track music practice, record sessions, and review your progress.

## Information Stored by the App

The app stores your practice information on your device, including folders, exercises, session history, notes, tags, tempo settings, routines, preferences, and optional audio recordings.

The app does not require an account and does not send this information to the developer or to a developer-operated server.

## Microphone Access

Microphone access is used only when you choose to record your practice. Starting a practice timer does not automatically start recording.

You can manage microphone permission in your device’s Settings.

## Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. The developer does not sell your practice information.

## Importing, Exporting, and Sharing

Information is imported, exported, or shared when you choose those actions.

CSV exports include practice records but do not include audio recordings, daily notes, saved routines, or app settings. A CSV export is not a complete app backup.

Files you share or save to another service are subject to that service’s privacy practices.

## Siri and Shortcuts

If you use the app’s Siri or Shortcuts actions, Apple processes those interactions according to its privacy policies. These actions do not send your practice information to a developer-operated server.

## Device Backups

Depending on your device settings, app information may be included in backups managed by Apple. These backups are governed by Apple’s policies and your backup settings.

## Deleting Information

Settings → Data → Delete Practice Records removes folders, exercises, sessions, and daily notes from the app on that device. It does not reset all app settings.

Recordings linked to deleted sessions will no longer be accessible through those sessions. This action does not delete copies you previously exported, shared, or saved in device backups.

You can remove the app and its local app data using Delete App in your device’s Settings. Offloading the app preserves its documents and data.

## Children’s Privacy

The app does not require personal registration or knowingly collect children’s personal information on a developer-operated server.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or privacy practices change. The effective date above will reflect the latest revision.
