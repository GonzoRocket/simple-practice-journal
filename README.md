# Simple Practice Journal — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026  
Publisher: GonzoRocket

Simple Practice Journal helps you organize music practice, record sessions, and review your progress. This policy covers the iPhone/iPad and Android apps. Platform-specific behavior is identified below.

## Information stored by the app

The app stores your practice information on your device, including folders, exercises, session history, notes, tags, tempo settings, routines, preferences, daily reflections, and optional audio recordings. No account is required.

The app does not send your practice information or recordings to the developer or a developer-operated server. The Android app does not request Internet permission.

## Microphone access

Microphone permission is optional and is used only when you choose to record practice. Starting a practice timer does not automatically start recording. Denying microphone permission does not prevent timing, notes, or progress tracking. You can manage permission in your device's settings.

On Android, recording and playback stop when the app moves into the background. The practice timer continues until you pause or finish it.

## Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The apps do not include advertising, developer-operated analytics, third-party tracking SDKs, or cross-app tracking. The developer does not sell your practice information.

## Importing, exporting, and external services

You choose which files to import and where to save or share exports. Android uses the system document picker, which gives the app access to the file or destination you select.

CSV exports include folders, exercises, practice sessions, session notes, tags, and BPM goals. They do not include audio files, daily reflections, saved routines, or preferences. A CSV export is a partial copy of practice records, not a complete app backup. It may contain private notes.

Files you save or share through another service are subject to that service's privacy practices. Opening a support link, privacy-policy link, or email contact launches your chosen browser or app; that service processes the interaction according to its own policies.

## Apple Siri and Shortcuts

If you use the iOS app's Siri or Shortcuts actions, Apple processes those interactions according to its privacy policies. The Android app does not implement these actions. Neither app sends those interactions to a developer-operated server.

## Device backups

Depending on your device settings, app information may be included in backups or device transfers managed by Apple, Android, Google, or your selected backup provider. Android's configured backup rules include journal data and recordings, subject to platform size limits and provider settings. Recording references are stored so that Android can resolve them within the app's restored private storage.

The developer does not operate or have access to these backups. Manage backup copies through your device or backup provider. Backups are separate from CSV export and may not include every file.

## Retention and deletion

Your local data is retained until you delete it or clear/uninstall the app.

**Android:** Settings → Delete All Local Data removes this installation's folders, exercises, sessions, notes, daily reflections, routines, preferences, and audio recording files after confirmation. You can also use Clear storage or Uninstall in Android Settings.

**iPhone/iPad:** Settings → Data → Delete Practice Records removes folders, exercises, sessions, and daily notes from the app on that device. It does not reset all preferences. Recordings linked to deleted sessions are no longer accessible through those sessions. Delete App in device settings removes the app and its local app data; offloading preserves its documents and data.

Deleting local data does not delete copies you previously exported, shared, or saved in device backups. Remove those copies separately through the service or backup provider. There is no developer-held app account to delete.

## Children's privacy

The apps do not require personal registration and do not knowingly collect children's personal information on a developer-operated server.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact GonzoRocket through the [project's support page](https://github.com/GonzoRocket/simple-practice-journal/issues). Do not include recordings, private practice notes, or sensitive personal information in public issues.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. The effective date above will reflect the latest revision.
